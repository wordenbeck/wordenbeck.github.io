How it works

How to Play

  1. Spin the slot machine to draw an identity card — a role, three AI skills, three human strengths.
  2. Discuss as a group for 5, 10 or 15 minutes: where does AI help, where do humans win?
  3. Reveal the AI perspective — a short scenario from 2030 and one reflection question.
  4. Spin again — new role, new conversation.
